Giant bluefin, twenty miles from Plymouth.

Ten hours offshore with a crew that has tagged hundreds of Atlantic bluefin, up to 935 lb.

Big game

A fishery that's come home

Crusader and her skippers and crew have been tagging and releasing giant Atlantic bluefin tuna every CHART season. Beyond leading this fishery out of Plymouth, the work feeds the management and stock recovery of an iconic species through careful handling and tagging. We hold tags for NOAA and will keep tagging these fish for research.

Fishing is from our custom-built fighting chair or stand-up, using lures and baits. We cater for every level of experience, with all kit free of charge and full tuition throughout.

How the day runs

  1. Early
    Meet at the BarbicanBoard at the Events Pontoon or Mount Batten. Safety briefing and a run-through of the chair.
  2. Run out
    Offshore to the fishCrusader is fast, so more of your ten hours is spent fishing.
  3. Fish
    Lures and baitsThe crew read the birds, bait and water. You take the rod when it goes.
  4. Hook-up
    Chair or stand-upCoached through the fight by the crew, however long it takes.
  5. Release
    Measure, tag, releaseEvery fish handled in the water and tagged for science. Photos boatside.

Before you book

Good to know

Where do we meet?

Crusader picks up from the Barbican Events Pontoon and the Mount Batten Pontoon, both in Plymouth.

Do I need my own kit?

No. Full kit is provided at no extra cost on every trip, with tuition from the crew.

Can I take fish home?

On reef, wreck and banks trips, fish kept for the pot are dispatched, cleaned and stored in the on-deck ice hold. Sharks and tuna are always released.

What's on board?

Inside and outside seating, a hot water boiler, microwave and a clean deck-entry toilet.

What happens if the weather turns?

Long-range trips are only scheduled when the weather and tides allow. Concept placeholder: the live site would state the rebooking policy from the T&Cs here.

Overview

Crusader Charters, as it stands today

Taken from the live site at crusader-marine.co.uk (GoDaddy Website Builder). Colours and fonts are pulled from the site's own CSS, not judged by eye. Every photo in the concept is theirs.

01 · Logo

A wordmark, no symbol

There is no logo file. The brand is "Crusader Charters ltd" typed in Yeseva One, all caps, black on the gold header. The concept keeps that face and tidies the lock-up: CRUSADER as the hero word, "Charters · Plymouth" as a spaced sub-line, and drops "ltd" from public use.

03 · Type

Three faces, used loosely

Yeseva One

Logo only. Strong, maritime-classic. Keep and promote to all headlines.

Lato

Hero and section titles, often all caps at 22px. Replace with Libre Franklin for body and UI.

Lusitana

Buttons and nav. Competes with Yeseva. Drop; add IBM Plex Mono for prices, distances and dates.

04 · Photography

Real, on-deck, and the best asset they have

Proper catch shots on Crusader's deck, open blue water, tuna boatside in white water, and underwater shark shots. Unstaged and credible. On the live site it's mostly shown as 70px gallery thumbnails and repeated card images.

05 · Tone of voice

Knowledgeable and proud, buried under SEO phrasing

When the skipper's voice comes through it's confident, expert and conservation-minded. The problem is "Plymouth charter boats" and "Plymouth deep sea fishing" being forced into nearly every paragraph.

"Crusader is a purpose-built 38' Evolution, maintained to the highest standards; she is spacious, comfortable, and fast."Our Story · keep
"Over the past three CHART tagging seasons, Crusader has tagged hundreds of tuna, reaching an immense size of 110" and 935lb!"Big Game Bluefin Tuna · lead with this
"Come join the original and best shark swim operator from Plymouth, known for our exceptional Plymouth fishing charters."Shark Swims · the keyword habit
"In 2024, we will utilize a range of techniques, including lures and baits…"Tuna · dated copy
06 · Read

What's working, what's holding it back

Working

  • Credentials most charter boats would kill for: since 2007, WISE accredited, NOAA and CHART tagging, a 935 lb bluefin, TripAdvisor top 10% worldwide.
  • Prices are published, which builds trust and cuts tyre-kickers.
  • Genuine photography of real customers and real fish.
  • A clear conservation stance (100% shark catch and release).
  • WhatsApp and an events list with live space counts.

Holding it back

  • One long GoDaddy template page; trips, prices and story all run together.
  • Booking is a mailto link to a personal Gmail plus a PDF of T&Cs to print and sign.
  • Two emails, two phone numbers and a separate safari site split the enquiry path.
  • Keyword-stuffed copy reads robotic and undersells the expertise.
  • Stale details ("In 2024…"), a typo'd link, events showing "All day" for evening trips.
  • The big story, giant tuna on your doorstep, is fourth down the page.
07 · Concept direction

Keep, elevate, show off

Keep

  • Yeseva One wordmark
  • Sand gold #DDCD84
  • Their story, prices and photos, word for word where possible
  • Phone, WhatsApp, pontoons

Elevate

  • Hull navy as the anchor colour, gold as the highlight
  • Yeseva for headlines, Libre Franklin body, mono for numbers
  • Full-bleed photography instead of thumbnails
  • Separate pages for trips and tuna; copy rewritten for people, not keywords

Lead with

  • Big game first: bluefin and sharks are what makes Crusader different from every other Barbican boat
  • Conservation as proof of expertise, not a footnote
